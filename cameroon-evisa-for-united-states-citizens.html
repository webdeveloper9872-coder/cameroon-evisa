Cameroon e-Visa for United States Citizens

United States citizens must obtain an approved Cameroon e-Visa prior to boarding flights from the United States to Douala or Yaoundé.

Visa Eligibility & Categories for US Passport Holders

US citizens are eligible to apply online for all Types of Cameroon eVisa, including Short-Stay (up to 6 months), Express Short-Stay, Long-Stay (6 months to 1 year), and Transit permits.

  • US tourists and family visitors typically select the 0–6 Month Short-Stay eVisa (Single or Multiple entry).
  • American corporate executives traveling to Douala can choose the Express Short-Stay eVisa for priority review.
  • All online approvals issue a QR pre-authorization that converts into a physical visa sticker upon arrival in Cameroon.

Key Requirements for United States Applicants

Minimum 6 Months

US Passport Validity

Your United States passport must remain valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned entry date into Cameroon.

CDC Yellow Card Required

Yellow Fever Immunization

An International Certificate of Vaccination (Yellow Card) is mandatory for all US travelers entering Cameroon.

Round-Trip Itinerary

Flight & Lodging Proof

Confirmed return airfare and hotel reservation or legalized host invitation in Cameroon.

Document Checklist for American Travelers

Before uploading files, US applicants should verify that their digital scans satisfy all Cameroon Visa Requirements.

  • Color scan of the US passport bio-data page and a recent 2x2 or standard passport photo.
  • Recent bank statement showing sufficient funds for the stay in Cameroon.
  • Business letter from the US employer and host invitation in Cameroon for commercial visits.

Fees & Processing Timelines for US Citizens

Standard processing takes 9 to 11 working days, while Rush (7–9 working days) and Super Rush (5–7 working days) tiers accommodate tighter schedules. Full cost tables are published under Cameroon eVisa Fees.

  • Short-Stay eVisa (0–6 Months): $202.00 USD government fee + $169.00 USD service fee ($371.00 USD total).
  • Express Short-Stay eVisa: $300.00 USD government fee + $169.00 USD service fee ($469.00 USD total).
  • Long-Stay eVisa (6M–1 Year): $397.00 USD government fee + $169.00 USD service fee ($566.00 USD total).
