Cameroon e-visa for Canadian Citizens

Canadian passport holders visiting Cameroon for tourism, family visits, academic cooperation, or business must obtain a Cameroon e-visa online before departure.

Visa Options for Canadian Travelers

Because both Canada and Cameroon share English and French as official languages, Canadian visitors frequently travel for family visits, academic exchanges, and holiday tours under the Cameroon Tourist eVisa or Short-Stay category.

  • Short-Stay eVisa (0 to 6 Months): Single or multiple entry for tourism, family visits, or business.
  • Express Short-Stay eVisa (0 to 6 Months): Accelerated review for urgent Canadian departures.
  • Long-Stay eVisa (6 Months to 1 Year): For Canadian researchers, NGO staff, and expatriate workers.

Visiting Family & Relatives in Cameroon

Canadians traveling to stay with relatives in Yaoundé, Douala, or Bafoussam should review Cameroon Family Visit eVisa requirements to ensure their host’s lodging certificate carries the proper municipal stamp.

  • Choose Standard (9–11 working days), Rush (7–9 working days), or Super Rush (5–7 working days) processing.

  • Biometric fingerprinting and visa sticker placement are completed on arrival in Cameroon.
