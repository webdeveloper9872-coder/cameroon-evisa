Health and Vaccination Requirements for Traveling to Cameroon

Compliance with Cameroon’s public health regulations—led by mandatory Yellow Fever vaccination—is required both for online eVisa approval and airport border entry.

Compulsory Yellow Fever Vaccination (ICVP)

An International Certificate of Vaccination or Prophylaxis (ICVP) proving Yellow Fever immunization is a mandatory upload among the Cameroon Visa Requirements.

  • Must be administered at least 10 days before arrival in Cameroon for primary immunization immunity.
  • A single lifetime dose of the Yellow Fever vaccine is recognized under WHO International Health Regulations.
  • Port health officers inspect the original yellow booklet upon arrival at Douala and Yaoundé airports.

Prescription antimalarial medication, DEET insect repellent, and comprehensive medical evacuation insurance.

Recommended Vaccinations & Malaria Prophylaxis

Alongside immunizations, visitors are strongly encouraged to arrange medical coverage as outlined in Travel Insurance for Visiting Cameroon.

  • Consult a travel medicine clinic 4 to 6 weeks prior to departure for antimalarial prescriptions.
  • Use mosquito nets and EPA-approved insect repellents, particularly in lowland and coastal areas.
  • Pack a personal travel health kit with oral rehydration salts and prescription medications in original labeled containers.

Seasonal Health Planning & Emergency Readiness

Weather conditions affect mosquito activity and heat exposure across regions; review seasonal patterns in When to Visit Cameroon when packing medical supplies.

  • Drink only bottled, boiled, or filtered water throughout your stay in Cameroon.
  • Keep digital and printed copies of your Yellow Fever certificate and insurance policy with your passport.
  • Major hospitals and private clinics are located in Yaoundé (Centre) and Douala (Littoral).
