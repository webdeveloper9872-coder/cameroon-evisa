Republic of Cameroon · Electronic Visa Overview

Cameroon eVisa

International visitors traveling to the Republic of Cameroon obtain pre-travel electronic visa authorization online prior to boarding flights bound for Douala or Yaoundé.

Short-Stay: 0–6 Months · Long-Stay: 6M–1 Year · Standard Review: 9–11 Working Days

Cameroon Visa Types & Stay Durations

Selecting the appropriate authorization depends on your travel purpose and intended stay length. Travelers can review the full classification under Types of Cameroon eVisa to match their itinerary with single-entry or multiple-entry validity.

  • Short-Stay eVisa: Issued for tourism, business meetings, or family visits up to 6 months.
  • Express Short-Stay eVisa: Priority review channel for urgent departures with up to 6 months validity.
  • Long-Stay eVisa: Designed for academic study, employment contracts, or extended residency from 6 months to 1 year.
  • Transit eVisa: Covers short airport or land layovers for 1 to 4 days or over 4 days.

Cameroon eVisa Requirements & Eligibility

All foreign nationals—except citizens of visa-exempt CEMAC and bilateral partner states such as Central African Republic, Chad, Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, and Nigeria—must hold an approved electronic authorization before arrival. Detailed document specifications are outlined under Cameroon Visa Requirements.

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the planned entry date into Cameroon.
  • Confirmed round-trip or onward flight reservation arriving at Douala (DLA) or Yaoundé Nsimalen (NSI).
  • International Certificate of Vaccination proving Yellow Fever immunization.
  • Proof of accommodation (hotel booking or certified host invitation) and recent passport-size photograph.

Cameroon Travel Preparation & Biometric Sticker Issuance

Once the online submission is approved, travelers receive a QR-coded electronic visa authorization via email. Upon landing at Douala International Airport or Yaoundé Nsimalen International Airport, border officers capture biometric fingerprints and affix the physical visa sticker directly into the passport.

  • Standard Processing: 9 to 11 working days for routine pre-travel review.
  • Rush Processing: 7 to 9 working days for accelerated verification.
  • Super Rush Processing: 5 to 7 working days for urgent departures.

Private Assistance vs Government Application

Travelers may apply either through an independent visa assistance service or directly via the Cameroon government portal.

Independent Visa Assistance Service

  • Operates as a private, independent third-party service and is not affiliated with the Government of Cameroon.
  • Provides manual document formatting checks, application review, and WhatsApp/phone customer support.
  • Charges an additional service fee ($69 to $169 USD) on top of the statutory government visa fee.
  • Does not control government immigration decisions, visa approvals, or official processing speed.

Direct Government Application

  • Submitted directly by the traveler through the state immigration system without third-party review.
  • Applicants manage their own document sizing, formatting, and form entries independently.
  • Charges only the statutory government visa fee without an added private assistance fee.
  • All final visa issuance decisions and airport biometric checks rest solely with Cameroonian authorities.
