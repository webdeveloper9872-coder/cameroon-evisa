Cameroon e-visa for the United Kingdom

British citizens traveling from the United Kingdom require an approved Cameroon e-visa before departing for Douala or Yaoundé.

Visa Categories Available to British Citizens

Holders of British citizen passports can apply online for a Cameroon Short Stay eVisa for stays up to 6 months, or select Transit and Long-Stay options depending on their travel purpose.

  • Tourism & Commonwealth Heritage Visits: Explore Limbe, Buea, Bamenda, and Waza National Park for up to 6 months.
  • Commercial & Energy Sector Visits: Attend corporate meetings in Douala with single or multiple entry.
  • Family Visits: Stay with relatives in Cameroon using a municipally legalized host certificate.

UK Applicant Checklist & Entry Standards

6+ Months Validity

British Passport Scan

Clear scan of the UK passport photo page with at least 6 months validity from arrival and two blank pages.

Confirmed Bookings

Accommodation & Flights

Return flight confirmation from London, Manchester, or European hubs plus hotel or host proof.

ICVP & Bank Statement

Health & Financial Proof

Yellow Fever vaccination certificate and a recent UK bank statement showing travel funds.

Processing Times for United Kingdom Applications

UK travelers should submit their online request well ahead of departure. Detailed review windows are outlined under Cameroon eVisa Processing Time.

  • Standard Review: 9 to 11 working days.
  • Rush Review: 7 to 9 working days.
  • Super Rush Review: 5 to 7 working days.

Arrival & Biometric Visa Sticker Collection

Ensure all uploaded records comply with Cameroon Visa Requirements. Upon landing at Douala or Yaoundé Nsimalen Airport, present your printed QR authorization to complete biometric scanning and receive the visa sticker.

  • Short-Stay eVisa fee for UK citizens is $202.00 USD government cost plus $169.00 USD service fee ($371.00 USD total).
  • Keep printed copies of your Yellow Fever card and hotel confirmation in your carry-on bag.
  • Verify that your passport expiration date extends at least 180 days beyond arrival.
