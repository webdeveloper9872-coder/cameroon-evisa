Types of Cameroon eVisa

Cameroon structures its electronic travel authorizations by stay duration, entry frequency, and travel purpose so visitors select the exact category matching their itinerary.

Short-Term & Express Visa Classifications

Visitors entering Cameroon for up to six months apply under the Cameroon Short Stay eVisa framework. Both single-entry and multiple-entry permissions are available for leisure, cultural visits, and commercial meetings.

  • Tourist Visits: Sightseeing in Waza National Park, Kribi, Limbe, and Mount Cameroon.
  • Business Engagements: Attending trade fairs, contract negotiations, and corporate audits in Douala.
  • Family & Social Visits: Staying with relatives or legal residents in Cameroonian municipalities.
  • Express Short-Stay Option: Fast-tracked review for urgent short-term departures.

Comparison of Cameroon eVisa Categories

Validity: Up to 6 Months

Short-Stay eVisa

Available in single or multiple entry formats for tourism, family visits, and short business trips.

Validity: Up to 6 Months · Priority

Express Short-Stay eVisa

Provides accelerated processing while preserving the same 6-month stay window and permitted activities.

Validity: 6 Months to 1 Year

Long-Stay eVisa

Required for university studies, salaried employment, research fellowships, and long-term residence.

Validity: 1–4 Days or Over 4 Days

Transit eVisa

Issued to passengers connecting through Cameroonian airports or land borders to a third country.

Extended Stay & Transit Authorizations

Travelers planning stays exceeding 180 days must obtain a Cameroon Long-Stay eVisa supported by employment contracts or academic admission letters.

  • Validity begins on the date of issuance, whereas stay duration starts upon border entry.
  • Single-entry visas expire once the traveler exits Cameroon, regardless of remaining days.
  • Multiple-entry visas permit repeated entries during the approved validity window.

Matching Documentation to Visa Category

Every visa category requires core identity records alongside purpose-specific proofs outlined in Cameroon Visa Requirements. Submitting accurate supporting files prevents category reclassification delays.

  • Verify passport validity covers at least 6 months past the intended entry date.
  • Ensure host invitations or hotel confirmations match the exact dates on flight itineraries.
  • Print the approved QR authorization notice for inspection at airport boarding gates.
