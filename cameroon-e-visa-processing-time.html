Cameroon eVisa Processing Time

Cameroon eVisa processing time refers to the working days required to verify your online application and issue the electronic pre-approval notice prior to departure.

Three Processing Speed Options

Travelers can choose from three processing speeds when submitting their online application. Cost differences across visa categories are detailed under Cameroon eVisa Fees.

  • Standard Processing: 9 to 11 working days — recommended for travelers applying at least 3 weeks ahead.
  • Rush Processing: 7 to 9 working days — designed for departures within two weeks.
  • Super Rush Processing: 5 to 7 working days — priority review for urgent travel schedules.

Factors That Influence Review Duration

Incomplete scans or mismatched itinerary dates can extend review times. Applicants should verify their uploads against Cameroon Visa Requirements before submission.

  • Public Holidays & Weekends: Working days exclude Cameroonian national holidays and weekends.
  • Document Legibility: Blurry passport bio pages or missing Yellow Fever certificates trigger manual follow-up.
  • Seasonal Volume: Peak travel months (November–January and July–August) experience higher application volume.

From Online Pre-Approval to Airport Sticker

Travelers needing the fastest short-stay turnaround can select the Cameroon Express Visa for Short Stay.

  • Once approved, the electronic authorization with a QR code is delivered directly by email.
  • Print the authorization notice and present it at airline check-in and upon arrival in Cameroon.
  • Border officers at Douala or Yaoundé airport capture biometrics and print the visa sticker on arrival.
