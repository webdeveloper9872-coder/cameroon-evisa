Cameroon Visa Requirements

Preparing legible digital scans of mandatory travel, health, and financial records ensures smooth verification for your Cameroon electronic visa.

Core Identity & Travel Document Checklist

Every applicant submitting an online request for a Cameroon eVisa must provide clear color scans of their primary travel documents.

  • Valid Passport: Bio-data page showing at least 6 months validity from arrival and two blank pages.
  • Digital Photograph: Recent color passport photo matching Cameroon Visa Photo Requirements.
  • Flight Itinerary: Confirmed round-trip or onward ticket showing entry and exit dates.
  • Accommodation Proof: Confirmed hotel reservation or legalized certificate of lodging from a host in Cameroon.

Purpose-Specific Supporting Documents

Short-Stay Leisure

Tourism & Leisure Files

Confirmed hotel bookings, day-by-day travel itinerary, and recent bank statements confirming sufficient travel funds.

Corporate Travel

Business & Commercial Files

Signed invitation letter from a Cameroon-registered partner company, business registration extract, and employer dispatch letter.

Private & Extended Stay

Family & Long-Stay Files

Municipal-certified invitation letter, host national ID or residence permit copy, and employment or university enrollment contract.

Mandatory Health & Financial Records

Public health compliance is strictly verified both during online review and at airport health desks. Review full medical protocols under Health and Vaccination Requirements for Traveling to Cameroon.

  • Yellow Fever Certificate: International Certificate of Vaccination (ICVP) is compulsory for all travelers.
  • Financial Solvency: Recent bank statements or sponsorship guarantee covering the stay duration.
  • Minors Documentation: Birth certificate and notarized parental authorization if traveling with one parent or alone.

Document Formatting & Submission Standards

Uploaded files should be clear, uncropped, and free of glare so immigration officers can verify all machine-readable passport zones and official stamps.

  • Ensure all names and passport numbers match identically across flight tickets and invitations.
  • Keep physical originals of all uploaded documents in carry-on luggage for arrival inspection.
  • Verify municipal legalization stamps on private accommodation certificates issued in Cameroon.
