When to Visit Cameroon

Stretching from the Gulf of Guinea to the Lake Chad basin, Cameroon spans equatorial, highland, and Sudano-Sahelian climate zones with distinct travel seasons.

Peak Dry Season: November to February

The nationwide dry window between November and February is the most popular period for wildlife safaris and volcanic ascents. Travelers visiting during peak winter months should apply early for their Cameroon eVisa.

  • Ideal conditions for summiting Mount Cameroon in Buea with clear morning skies.
  • Best wildlife visibility around waterholes in Waza and Bénoué National Parks.
  • Comfortable sunshine along the beaches of Kribi and Limbe.

Cameroon Climate Calendar by Season

Primary Dry Season · Peak Travel

November – February

Sunny skies, drier dirt roads, and optimal conditions for trekking, safaris, and coastal holidays.

Shoulder Transition · Lush Landscapes

March – May

Warm temperatures in the north and early tropical showers in the south that refresh waterfalls and forests.

Green Rainy Season · Rainforest Peak

June – October

Heavy monsoon rains along the coast and western highlands; Lobé and Ekom Nkam waterfalls reach peak flow.

Regional Microclimates: Coast vs. Savanna

While southern rainforests around the Dja Reserve experience two rainy periods, the northern savanna has a single wet season from June to September. Compare regional highlights in Top destinations in Cameroon.

  • Coastal Zone (Douala, Limbe, Kribi): Warm and humid year-round (24°C–31°C); driest from December to February.
  • Western Highlands (Bamenda, Dschang, Buea): Cooler mountain air (15°C–25°C) with crisp evenings.
  • Northern Savanna (Garoua, Maroua): Dry and sunny from November to April; hot before the summer rains.

Aligning Your Visa & Health Preparation with Travel Dates

Regardless of season, all visitors must comply with Health and Vaccination Requirements for Traveling to Cameroon, including Yellow Fever immunization at least 10 days before arrival.

  • Submit your online visa application 2 to 3 weeks before peak December and January travel dates.
  • Pack waterproof gear if visiting Korup or Debundscha during the green season.
  • Schedule domestic flights between Yaoundé and Garoua/Maroua when traveling to northern parks.
