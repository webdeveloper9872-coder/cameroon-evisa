Cameroon Short Stay eVisa

The Cameroon Short Stay eVisa authorizes temporary visits of up to 6 months for leisure travelers, business delegates, and family visitors.

Permitted Activities Under Short-Stay Authorization

This category consolidates temporary non-immigrant travel into a single 0-to-6-month electronic permit. Vacationers primarily apply under the Cameroon Tourist eVisa purpose when exploring national parks and coastal resorts.

  • Leisure & Sightseeing: Independent holiday travel, eco-tours, and coastal stays in Kribi and Limbe.
  • Commercial Meetings: Short-term trade consultations and conferences without local employment.
  • Family & Cultural Visits: Visiting relatives, attending weddings, or participating in cultural events.

Key Specifications of the Short-Stay eVisa

0 to 6 Months

Maximum Stay Window

Permits stays up to 180 days from the date of entry at Douala or Yaoundé international airports.

Single or Multiple Entry

Entry Flexibility

Choose single entry for one-off trips or multiple entry for regional travel across Central Africa.

$371.00 USD Total

Standard Fee Structure

Comprises $202.00 USD government visa fee and $169.00 USD application service fee.

Short-Stay Documentary Requirements

Corporate visitors attending conferences should review Cameroon Business eVisa documentation rules to ensure their host company invitation letter is properly formatted.

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond arrival with two blank visa pages.
  • Confirmed return flight ticket and verified hotel booking or legalized host certificate.
  • Valid Yellow Fever vaccination card and recent color passport photograph.

Processing Options & Extension Rules

Standard short-stay applications are processed in 9 to 11 working days, with Rush (7–9 working days) and Super Rush (5–7 working days) upgrades available. Travelers requiring extra time in the country can review Cameroon eVisa Extension Information before their initial authorized stay expires.

  • Submit applications at least 2 to 3 weeks prior to scheduled departure.
  • Biometric enrollment and physical visa sticker placement occur upon airport arrival.
  • Overstaying beyond the authorized period incurs administrative penalties at departure.
